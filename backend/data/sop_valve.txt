SOP: Valve Inspection Procedure

Purpose:
This procedure describes the standard process for inspecting industrial valves.

Inspection Steps:
1. Ensure the equipment is safely isolated before inspection.
2. Check the valve body for visible damage, cracks, or corrosion.
3. Inspect the valve connections for leakage.
4. Verify that the valve opens and closes correctly.
5. Record all inspection findings.
6. Report any abnormal condition to the maintenance supervisor.

Safety:
Always follow the required safety and isolation procedures before beginning inspection.

Approval:
Inspection findings must be reviewed and approved by the responsible supervisor.
