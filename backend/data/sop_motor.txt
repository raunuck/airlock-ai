Motor Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure

Purpose:

To ensure safe and reliable operation of industrial motors.

Maintenance Steps:

1. Switch off the motor before beginning maintenance.
2. Isolate the motor from all electrical energy sources.
3. Check the motor casing for visible damage or cracks.
4. Inspect electrical connections for loose or damaged wires.
5. Check motor bearings for unusual noise or vibration.
6. Record all maintenance findings.
7. Report abnormal conditions to the maintenance supervisor.

Safety:

Always follow electrical isolation and safety procedures before maintenance.

Approval:

Motor maintenance findings must be reviewed by the responsible supervisor.
