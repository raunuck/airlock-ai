SOP: Equipment Safety Procedure

Purpose:
This procedure defines the basic safety requirements for working with industrial equipment.

Safety Steps:
1. Wear the required personal protective equipment before starting work.
2. Ensure the equipment is switched off before inspection or maintenance.
3. Isolate the equipment from all energy sources.
4. Place warning signs around the work area.
5. Do not operate equipment while maintenance work is in progress.
6. Report unsafe conditions to the safety supervisor.

Emergency:
In case of an emergency, stop the work immediately and inform the responsible supervisor.

Approval:
All safety incidents and observations must be recorded and reviewed.
