Compressor Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure

Purpose:

To ensure safe and reliable operation of industrial compressors.

Maintenance Steps:

1. Switch off the compressor before beginning maintenance.
2. Isolate the compressor from all energy sources.
3. Check the compressor casing for cracks or visible damage.
4. Inspect connections for leakage.
5. Check oil and lubrication levels.
6. Record all maintenance findings.
7. Report abnormal conditions to the maintenance supervisor.

Safety:

Always follow equipment isolation and safety procedures before maintenance.

Approval:

Maintenance findings must be reviewed by the responsible supervisor.
