Pump Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure

Purpose:
To ensure safe and reliable operation of industrial pumps.

Maintenance Steps:
1. Switch off the pump before beginning maintenance.
2. Isolate the pump from all energy sources.
3. Check the pump casing for cracks or visible damage.
4. Inspect the pump connections for leakage.
5. Check the lubrication level.
6. Record all maintenance findings.
7. Report abnormal conditions to the maintenance supervisor.

Safety:
Always follow equipment isolation and safety procedures before maintenance.

Approval:
Maintenance findings must be reviewed by the responsible supervisor.
